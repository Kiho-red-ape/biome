## [0:00–0:30] Intro

"This is a quick walkthrough of Biome — what it is, who it's for, and how each side of the platform works.

Biome is an operations layer for research studies. If you're running a study, Biome handles recruitment, screening, milestone tracking, compliance, and compensation — so you don't have to build any of that yourself.

There are three roles on the platform: Researcher, Research Partner, and Partner. I'll walk through each one."

## [0:30–2:30] Flow 1: Researcher

[ACTION: Click the "Researcher" card]

"Let's start with the researcher flow — someone who has a protocol and needs a cohort.

**Step 1 — Get an estimate.**

Here you can see the cost estimator. Before you commit to anything, you enter your cohort size, study duration, and any sample logistics. It outputs a clear cost picture so you can budget accurately.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 2]

**Step 2 — Submit intake / post study.**

A short intake form captures your protocol, eligibility criteria, and milestones. No long forms — once you're done, it publishes directly to your study dashboard. Here you can see a draft study card with the title, category, and screening criteria already attached.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 3]

**Step 3 — Deposit budget.**

You fund the compensation pool upfront. This tells research partners the study is real and the compensation is secured. The funds are held in escrow and released against verified milestone completion — not on a promise.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 4]

**Step 4 — Recruit.**

This is OME — Biome's recruitment intelligence tool. You can see it here: rather than you spending time cold-calling hospitals or clinics, OME locates institutions suited to your study and provides compliance notes for each one. It does the legwork so you can focus on the science.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 5]

**Step 5 — Screen applicants.**

Once applications come in, you review them against your eligibility criteria and approve the cohort you want. You stay in full control of who joins — Biome doesn't make that decision for you.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 6]

**Step 6 — Run the study.**

Here you can see the live tracking view. As your cohort works through the protocol, you get real-time milestone and compliance data. Automated alerts flag anything at risk before it becomes a problem.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 7]

**Step 7 — Manage documents and compliance.**

Consent records, protocol versions, and your full audit trail are stored in one place — organized, versioned, and export-ready. When you need to produce a compliance bundle, it's already there.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 8]

**Step 8 — Payouts.**

On verified completion, research partners receive compensation end-to-end. Releases are per milestone, so every payout is tied to real participation — not just enrollment.

That's the full researcher flow. You bring the protocol; Biome handles operations so you can run it on your terms."

## [2:30–3:45] Flow 2: Research Partner

[ACTION: Click "← Back to roles", then click the "Research Partner" card]

"Now let's look at the research partner side — someone who joins studies and receives compensation for their participation.

**Step 1 — Discover studies.**

Here you can see a list of open studies matched to your profile. Each listing shows the protocol, time commitment, and compensation upfront — before you apply.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 2]

**Step 2 — Apply.**

One-click application. You review the protocol, confirm you meet the criteria, and submit. No cover letters, no back-and-forth.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 3]

**Step 3 — Get approved.**

The researcher reviews your eligibility and approves you into the cohort. You're notified the moment a slot is yours.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 4]

**Step 4 — Complete tasks.**

You work through milestones from home. Self-report tasks you submit yourself; researcher-confirmed tasks are verified on their end. Here you can see a milestone list with the current week's task ready to submit.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 5]

**Step 5 — Receive compensation.**

On verified completion, you receive compensation through your configured payout method. It's tied to milestones — so what you earn reflects what you actually completed.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 6]

**Step 6 — Build participation history.**

Every completed study becomes a credential on your profile. A strong completion record makes you a more attractive candidate for the studies you want to join next."

## [3:45–4:30] Flow 3: Partner

[ACTION: Click "← Back to roles", then click the "Partner" card]

"The third role is Partner — testing labs, clinics, and research organizations that support studies on the operational side.

**Step 1 — Join.**

You apply to the partner network. Engagements are per-study — no retainers, no bundled markups. Here you can see a sample partner application from a testing lab.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 2]

**Step 2 — List capabilities.**

You describe what you offer: assays, sample handling, turnaround times, capacity. This is what the network uses to match you to studies accurately.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 3]

**Step 3 — Receive matched studies.**

Qualified studies are surfaced to you based on your capabilities — no cold outreach. You review the scope and accept the ones that fit.

[ACTION: Progress to Step 4]

**Step 4 — Coordinate samples and data.**

You manage intake, processing, and structured data return per engagement. Each study has a clear, transparent scope — you always know exactly what's expected."
